How to play

  1. Aim: move the crosshair with your mouse or the arrow keys. On a phone, just tap.
  2. Cast: click, tap or press Space. Fish keep swimming while your line flies, so aim ahead of them.
  3. Decide: each fish carries an email. Choose Real or Phish before the timer runs out. Harder fish give you less time.
  4. Name the trick: if it's a phish, pick the trick the scammer used for 5 bonus points.
  5. Finish: catch 8 fish to end the round. Wrong answers and running out of time score 0. A hint costs 2 points.

Addresses you trust

    Don't make it easy for them. Scammers count on you being busy or distracted. Spotting phish is a small habit, and it only works if you repeat it. Come back tomorrow to keep your streak.

    What to do with a real phish

    1. Recognize the signs: urgent or emotional language, requests for personal or financial information, shortened links, and incorrect addresses or links.
    2. Resist clicking links or attachments, and report the message using your email app's report option.
    3. Delete it. Don't reply, and don't click "unsubscribe."

    If it might be real, don't use anything in the message. Type the company's site yourself, or contact the person another way.
